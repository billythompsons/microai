# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in MicroAI a harassment-free experience for everyone and to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards
Positive behavior includes empathy, respect, constructive feedback, accountability, and a focus on the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, publishing private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Community leaders may remove contributions that do not align with this Code. Report unacceptable behavior privately using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for reporters' privacy.

## Scope and attribution
This Code applies in all community spaces and when representing the community. Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
